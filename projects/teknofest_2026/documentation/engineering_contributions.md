# Engineering Contributions and Learning Outcomes

## Data preparation and annotation

My work included organizing image data, extracting frames from recorded videos and contributing to a large collaborative annotation effort with CVAT. This experience strengthened my understanding of data quality, consistency and the importance of careful labeling in computer vision projects.

## Software and hardware experience

I gained hands-on experience working with computer vision software, Python and Linux alongside camera hardware and embedded computers. The work reinforced the importance of debugging, documenting experiments and communicating across technical disciplines.

## Experimental engineering

I participated in repeated laboratory tests and physical measurements with the team. These activities helped me develop a more systematic approach to experimental work, troubleshooting and evaluating results.

## Collaboration and ownership

- Individual focus: computer vision and data preparation.
- Shared work: laboratory experiments and multidisciplinary development.
- Graphical user interface: developed by a teammate.

## Transferable skills

Data annotation, computer vision, experimental engineering, embedded computing, technical communication and collaborative problem-solving.

## Evidence and limitations

This retrospective is based on the author's account of the project. Exact dataset counts, performance metrics and test conditions are not independently verified here. Internal reports and operational materials are excluded from this public portfolio.
